suit: cups
name: Cups
domain: Feeling, relationship, intuition, and the inner life's tides.

---

Cups is the suit of what is felt and what passes between people: affection,
longing, grief, generosity, imagination, and the quieter sense of what
something means to you before you can say why. Where Swords asks what is true
and Wands asks what is wanted, Cups asks what it is like to be here. Every
card in the suit shows a vessel, and a vessel is a fair image for the suit's
whole subject: something that can be filled, offered, spilled, shared, or left
untouched.

The suit's fourteen cards (ten pips plus four court cards) follow feeling
through its stages, from the first welling-up of a new attachment, through
abundance and celebration, into disappointment, nostalgia, and the moment of
turning away from something that no longer nourishes, and on toward contentment
that has been earned rather than assumed. It is the suit most concerned with
relationships, though not only romantic ones. Friendship, family, creative
absorption, and the relationship a person has with their own moods all sit
inside its territory.

Traditionally paired with the element Water: emotion, receptivity, and
intuition, fluid and responsive to whatever contains it. This pairing is among
the most stable in the deck, and almost every tradition that assigns elements
at all agrees on it. It is a useful way to hold the suit in mind: Cups cards
are asking what is being felt, and what those feelings are asking for, more
than they are asking what should be done about them.
