The Sun is number 19, the fifth card of the third row (15 to 21), after the Moon and before Judgement.

One common reading takes it as clarity and warmth, things seen as they are. Following the Moon's uncertainty, it is the row's turn into daylight. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool arriving at simple confidence.
