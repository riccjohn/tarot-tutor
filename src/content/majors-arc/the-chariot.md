The Chariot is number 7, the last card of the first row (1 to 7), between the Lovers and Strength. Seven closes each row of the septenary scheme, so this card is the row's resolution.

One common reading takes it as directed will, forward movement that comes from holding opposing pulls in balance. As the end of the first row, it is the outer world mastered enough to steer. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool's first victory, before the more inward stretch that follows.
