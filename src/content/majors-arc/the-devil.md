The Devil is number 15, the first card of the third row (15 to 21), after Temperance and before the Tower. The third row is the last one, and this is where it opens.

One common reading takes the third row as the sequence's largest reckonings before it resolves, and the Devil as attachment: the habits and bargains a person has taken on and might loosen. It concerns what holds someone, not an outside evil. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool meeting his own binding patterns as the final stage begins.
