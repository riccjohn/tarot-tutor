The Empress is number 3, the third card of the first row (1 to 7), after the High Priestess and before the Emperor. Threes across the deck mark the point where a pairing meets the world, and here the third position is the row's first card of the tended, physical world.

One common reading takes her as growth, care and the making of things real. She and the Emperor at 4 are usually taken as a pair, one generative and one structuring. The narrative journey scheme keeps that pairing and reads them as the Fool's first parental figures.
