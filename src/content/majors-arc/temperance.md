Temperance is number 14, the last card of the second row (8 to 14), after Death and before the Devil. It closes the row the way the Chariot closes the first.

One common reading treats it as moderation, the patient mixing of unlike things into a workable whole. Coming right after an ending, it is the row's answer to change: adjust, combine, go slowly. The narrative journey scheme reads the same card as the Fool's integration before the final stretch.
