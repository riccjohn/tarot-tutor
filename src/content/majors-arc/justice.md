Justice is number 11, the fourth card of the second row (8 to 14), the middle of that row, between the Wheel of Fortune and the Hanged Man.

One common reading takes Justice as accountability and fair weighing, cause meeting consequence. After the Wheel's turning, it asks what a person is answerable for. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool taking stock partway along the road.
