The Hanged Man is number 12, the fifth card of the second row (8 to 14), after Justice and before Death. It shows a figure suspended in place while the world tips around him.

One common reading treats it as a voluntary pause and a change of perspective, seeing a situation by declining to act on it for a moment. In the row it is the step between a weighing and an ending. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool giving up a plan in order to see differently.
