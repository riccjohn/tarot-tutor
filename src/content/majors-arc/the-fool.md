The Fool is numbered 0 and sits outside the three rows of seven that organize the other twenty-one majors. Under the septenary scheme it belongs to no row: it comes before the sequence starts, and the rows are arranged around it.

One common reading treats a card with no number as a card with no fixed place, the open beginning before anything has been decided. That makes it a start rather than a stage. The other widely used scheme, the narrative journey, reads the Fool as the traveller and the twenty-one cards after it as the road; both schemes begin here.
