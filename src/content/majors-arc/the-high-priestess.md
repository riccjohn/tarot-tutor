The High Priestess is number 2, the second card of the first row (1 to 7), between the Magician and the Empress. Where 1 is a single actor, 2 is the first pairing, and she is the counterweight that comes straight after him.

One common reading sets her against the Magician's activity: he does, she waits and knows. That makes her the row's first turn toward what is unspoken and not yet explained. The narrative journey scheme reads her as an early lesson about what cannot be taught directly.
