The Emperor is number 4, the fourth card of the first row (1 to 7), directly after the Empress. Four is the number of a foundation, four sides and four corners, and this is where the row moves from making to organizing.

One common reading takes him as structure, authority and the rules a person builds or inherits. Set beside the Empress, he is the container to her growth. The narrative journey scheme reads the same card as the Fool's first meeting with external order.
