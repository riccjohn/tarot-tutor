The Moon is number 18, the fourth card of the third row (15 to 21), between the Star and the Sun. It is the middle of the row and the card of low light.

One common reading takes it as uncertainty and imagination, things seen only in part and not yet resolved. It is often paired with the Star before it as a shift from clear hope into murkier ground. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool's last stretch of doubt before the clearing.
