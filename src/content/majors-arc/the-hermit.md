The Hermit is number 9, the second card of the second row (8 to 14), after Strength and before the Wheel of Fortune. It is the row's withdrawal, a lantern-bearer standing apart from the crowd.

One common reading treats him as deliberate solitude, a search for one's own guidance in place of borrowed guidance. Following Strength, it is where the inward turn becomes a retreat from company. The narrative journey scheme reads the same card as the Fool's period of reflection.
