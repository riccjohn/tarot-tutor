Strength is number 8, the first card of the second row (8 to 14). It follows the Chariot, which closed the first row, and precedes the Hermit.

One common reading takes the second row as an inward turn: temper, patience, solitude, waiting, adjustment. Strength opens it as gentle, sustained influence in place of force, the quiet kind of courage, and the first step in working with one's own nature rather than the world outside it. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool learning self-command after a first victory.
