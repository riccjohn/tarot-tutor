Wheel of Fortune is number 10, the third card of the second row (8 to 14), between the Hermit and Justice. It is the first two-digit number in the sequence, and it falls near the middle of the twenty-one numbered majors.

One common reading takes it as cycles and turning points, the parts of a life that move on their own schedule. Coming straight after the Hermit's solitude, it marks the wider world's motion returning. The narrative journey scheme treats it as a hinge, the point where the story turns from self-directed to something larger.
