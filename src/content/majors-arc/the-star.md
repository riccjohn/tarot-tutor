The Star is number 17, the third card of the third row (15 to 21), after the Tower and before the Moon. It is the first quiet card after the sequence's most abrupt one.

One common reading treats it as renewed hope, an unhurried and steady openness after upheaval. It is often read as restoration that does not return things to how they were. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool recovering direction.
