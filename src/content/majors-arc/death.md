Death is number 13, the sixth card of the second row (8 to 14), between the Hanged Man and Temperance. In the sequence it is one stage among the rest, and it is about transformation.

One common reading takes it as ending, release and change: a chapter, a role or a way of seeing closes so that something else can begin. It sits just before the row's balancing card, Temperance, which suits an ending followed by recombination. The narrative journey scheme reads it as a threshold the Fool passes through, not a stopping point.
