The Tower is number 16, the second card of the third row (15 to 21), between the Devil and the Star. Where the Devil is a structure held in place, the Tower is a structure coming apart.

One common reading takes it as sudden upheaval: a foundation that turns out to be less stable than it looked, and the clearing that follows. Written plainly, it is about a break with something built on a shaky base. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool's sharpest reversal of expectation.
