Judgement is number 20, the sixth card of the third row (15 to 21), between the Sun and the World. It is the second-to-last major.

One common reading treats it as a calling and a review: looking back over what has been lived and deciding to answer it. Placed after the Sun, it turns clarity into a response. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool's reckoning with the road so far.
