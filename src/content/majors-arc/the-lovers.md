The Lovers is number 6, the sixth card of the first row (1 to 7), after the Hierophant and before the Chariot. The row is nearly complete, and this is the card where its inherited structures meet a personal decision.

One common reading centers on choice, with values made concrete by being chosen; it is often read as more about alignment than about romance. Sitting second to last in the row, it is a choice made just before the row's push forward. The narrative journey scheme frames it as the Fool's first real decision.
