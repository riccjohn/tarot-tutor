The Magician is number 1, the first card of the first row (1 to 7). It follows the Fool at 0 and opens the run that ends at the Chariot.

One common reading takes the first row as the outer, formative world: skills, teachers, family, structure, commitment. On that reading the Magician is the moment a beginning turns into intention, with the tools on the table and something about to be made from them. The narrative journey, the other common scheme, sees the same card as the Fool's first encounter with skill.
