The World is number 21, the last card of the third row (15 to 21) and the last of the majors. It follows Judgement and completes the third row, the way the Chariot and Temperance complete the first two.

One common reading takes it as completion and integration, a cycle finished and a person at home in it. Because the Fool at 0 stands outside the rows, the sequence can be read as circling back to that opening, so the end of the run is also a place to begin again. The narrative journey scheme reads it as the Fool's journey completed.
