The Hierophant is number 5, the fifth card of the first row (1 to 7), between the Emperor and the Lovers. Where the Emperor is a personal authority, this card is the institution: a shared teaching handed down.

One common reading treats him as tradition and belonging, the question of which inherited rules a person accepts. That suits his place in the row, since the next card is a choice and a choice needs something to choose against. The narrative journey scheme reads him as the Fool's schooling.
