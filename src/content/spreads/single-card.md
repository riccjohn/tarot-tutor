The simplest layout on the site, and the one most people reach for daily: one
card, drawn with no question attached to a position. The constraint is the
point. One card gives you one thing to sit with, where a larger spread gives
you relationships to untangle. It suits days when you want a prompt more than
an analysis.

Read the structure first, because that part is fixed: the suit, the number
and the image. What the card means to you today is the part you bring.
