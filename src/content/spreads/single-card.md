The simplest layout on the site, and the one most people reach for daily: a
single card, drawn without a question attached to a specific position. Its
value is in the constraint — one card means one thing to sit with, rather
than a spread's worth of relationships to untangle. Good for days you want a
prompt rather than an analysis.
