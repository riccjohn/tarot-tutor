    part. The five meanings are one workable set among those in circulation,
    and you are free to relabel the arms.

Five positions in a plus shape around a central card: the situation in the
middle, its foundation and crowning influence below and above, its recent past
and near-term development to either side.

It asks more of you than the three-card row, because the arms relate to the
center and not to each other in sequence. Read the center first. Then let each
arm answer one specific question about it, instead of reading all five as one
continuous story.
