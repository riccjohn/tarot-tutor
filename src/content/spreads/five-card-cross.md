Five positions arranged as a plus sign around a central card: the situation
in the middle, then its foundation and its near-term ceiling above and
below, and its recent past and near future to either side. More demanding to
read than the three-card layout because the positions relate to the center
rather than to each other in sequence — read the center card first, then let
each arm answer a specific question about it rather than reading all five as
one continuous story.
