name: The Magician
symbols:
    - element: A figure in a red robe over a white tunic raises a white wand in one hand while the other hand points down at the ground
      note: One arm reaches up and the other reaches down. Conventionally read as drawing from above and directing it below, with the figure as the point where the two meet.
    - element: A horizontal figure-eight floating above his head
      note: An unbroken loop with no start or end, the sign often called the lemniscate. Conventionally tied to continuity and to attention held steadily on one thing.
    - element: A table before him holding a cup, a sword, and a coin marked with a five-pointed star, with the wand raised in his hand as the fourth
      note: Four different objects, all laid out and within reach. Readers often connect them to the four suits, so that everything needed is already present and the question is what gets made from it.
    - element: Red roses hanging along the top edge and white lilies and more red roses growing thickly at the bottom
      note: The garden frames the figure above and below, in cultivated red and white. Often read as desire and purity, or as growth that has already begun around the work.
    - element: A flat, plain yellow background with a white headband on the figure
      note: Nothing else is in the scene, no landscape and no other people. The focus is narrowed to a single figure, a table, and one gesture.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the moment a beginning turns into intention. The tools are already on the table, the figure has one hand pointed up and one pointed down, and something is about to be made. It is often read as skill, focus, and the choice to begin working with what is available instead of waiting for more. What gets made, and whether it is used well, is not settled by the image.
invitationPrompt: Which of the things already within your reach is waiting for you to pick it up?
