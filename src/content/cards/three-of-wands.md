---
name: Three of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A figure seen entirely from behind, in a red robe with a green
          mantle over the shoulder and a thin band around the head
      note: With the face hidden, the viewer looks out along the figure's line
          of sight. The card is about what lies ahead, not about who is
          looking at it.
    - element: Three tall wooden staffs standing upright, budding with small
          green leaves, the figure's right hand resting on the nearest one
      note: The wands are planted, not carried. The Three's added element
          is already in the ground, and the hand on one staff suggests
          steadiness more than effort.
    - element: A flat, wide expanse of golden-yellow water below a plain
          ochre sky, with a low line of mountains at the horizon
      note: Nothing on the card is crowded or under pressure. The open
          horizon is the space the figure is looking into, a distance still
          to be crossed.
    - element: Two or three tiny ships on the water in the distance
      note: Often read as ventures already sent out, things set in motion
          that the figure is now watching from a distance rather than
          pushing along by hand.
    - element: A rough, rocky ledge of green and rust-red stone beneath the
          figure's feet
      note: The figure stands on high, uneven ground, at an outlook rather
          than a path. It fits a card about perspective and waiting for
          what has been launched.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the point where an idea
    has been set in motion and begins to reach beyond the person who started
    it. The figure has done the initial work and is now looking out to see
    what returns. In Wands that means a project, plan, or ambition that has
    taken visible shape and is beginning to have consequences in the wider
    world. It is a stage of foresight and patience as much as of ambition.
invitationPrompt: What have you already set in motion that you might simply
    watch for a while today?
