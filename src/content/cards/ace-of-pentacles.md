name: Ace of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: An open hand reaching in from a grey cloud at the left edge,
          palm up, with a large yellow coin resting on it
      note: The standard Ace composition. There is no figure behind the hand,
          only the cloud it emerges from, so the coin arrives as an offer with
          no giver attached to it.
    - element: A five-pointed star drawn inside the coin, within a double
          ring, with short rays radiating from behind the hand
      note: The pentacle is the suit's emblem, shown here alone and
          unattached to any situation. The rays mark the hand and the coin as
          the point of the card.
    - element: A green garden below, with a hedge of dark foliage dotted with
          red flowers and white lilies growing in front of it
      note: Conventionally read as cultivated ground. The garden is tended
          and already growing, which suits Pentacles' concern with what can
          be built and kept.
    - element: An arch of leaves set into the hedge, framing a pale blue
          mountain, with a yellow path running toward it
      note: The path leads through the arch to open country beyond the
          garden. It is often read as the direction the offer could lead, and
          it stays undecided, since nobody is yet walking it.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a concrete opening — an
    opportunity, a resource, or the first bit of groundwork for something
    that can be built. The coin is held out but not yet spent, so it is an
    offer more than an event. The garden below has already been tended and
    the path leads on through it, which is why this Ace is often read as
    potential with real ground to stand on. What the coin becomes depends on
    what the reader plants with it.
invitationPrompt: What is being offered to you right now, and what would it
    take to say yes to it or no?
