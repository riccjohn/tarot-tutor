---
name: The Moon
symbols:
    - element: A large yellow disc ringed with long rays, holding a crescent
          and a human face in profile, with the numeral XVIII above it
      note: The full disc and the crescent are drawn together in a single
          shape, so the light is shown both whole and partial at once. The
          profile face gives the light a watching quality.
    - element: Small yellow drops falling from the disc through the sky
          toward the ground
      note: The drops link the sky to the landscape below. They are often
          read as the moon's influence reaching down as feeling or
          imagination rather than as illumination.
    - element: Two grey towers, one on each edge of the card, each with a
          single small dark opening
      note: The towers work as a gateway that frames the scene. The path
          between them is the only route the card offers, and it leads
          away from the viewer into the hills.
    - element: An orange dog on the left and a tan wolf-like animal on the
          right, both facing up toward the moon with open mouths
      note: A tamed animal and a wild one respond to the same light in
          the same way. Conventionally read as the range of instinct in a
          single person, cultivated and uncultivated together.
    - element: A crayfish climbing out of a pool at the bottom edge, at the
          start of a winding pale path that runs between the animals to the
          horizon
      note: The crayfish is the lowest and oldest-looking figure on the card,
          and it is the one shown moving. It is usually read as something
          rising from the unconscious and heading, slowly, along the
          path.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading takes this card as uncertainty and
    imagination, things seen only in part and not yet resolved. The moon
    lights the scene without making every shape clear, so a person looking
    at it is asked to move by feel as well as sight. It is often paired with
    the Star before it, as a shift from clear hope into murkier ground, and
    with the Sun after it. Uncertain does not mean wrong here, and the
    path on the card still runs onward.
invitationPrompt: What is something you sense right now but cannot yet
    explain, and what would it be like to let it stay unexplained for a
    while?
