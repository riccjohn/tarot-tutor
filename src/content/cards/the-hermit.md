name: The Hermit
symbols:
    - element: A tall, hooded figure in a long grey cloak with a white beard, standing alone in profile with his head bowed
      note: He is the only figure in the picture and he looks down and to the side, not out at the viewer. Conventionally read as deliberate solitude and inward attention.
    - element: A black lantern raised in one hand at the edge of the card, its panes glowing yellow with short rays around it
      note: The light is small and held at arm's length, and it lights the space around him only a little. Often read as guidance that reaches a step or two ahead, and that he carries himself instead of following.
    - element: A long yellow staff planted at his side and held in the other hand
      note: The staff is taller than he is. Conventionally read as support for the walking, and as authority earned through experience rather than given by office.
    - element: A narrow band of snow and jagged white peaks along the bottom edge, with the figure standing on the ridge
      note: He stands at a height and there is no path marked. Often read as distance from others, and as a place reached only by going up alone.
    - element: A plain, empty blue-grey sky with no landscape, figures, or objects other than the man
      note: Nothing competes with the single light. The emptiness is part of the composition, and it is often read as quiet, or as a space in which nothing else is asking for attention.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as deliberate solitude, a search for one's own guidance in place of borrowed guidance. The lantern is small and carried by one person, and it lights the next stretch of the way rather than the whole route. After Strength, it is where the inward turn becomes a step back from company. What he finds, and when he returns, is not shown.
invitationPrompt: What would you listen for if you spent an hour with no one else's opinions in the room?
