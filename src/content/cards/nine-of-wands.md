name: Nine of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A figure in a rust-coloured tunic and pale boots, wearing a
          white band around the head, holding a single staff upright with
          both hands
      note: The grip is firm and close to the body. The figure holds one wand
          in hand while eight stand behind, so the Nine is made of what has
          been carried and what has been lined up.
    - element: Eight more staffs planted upright in a fence-like row behind
          the figure, four on either side of the centre
      note: The row reads as an accumulated wall of effort. Nearly the whole
          suit's cycle of wands is now standing behind one person, one short
          of the Ten.
    - element: A wary sideways look, with the eyes turned to the left of the
          card and the body angled the other way
      note: The figure is watching for something beyond the picture's frame.
          It is a stance of vigilance kept up after long effort, not of
          being caught off guard.
    - element: A flat grey ground at the bottom, and a low line of green hills
          under a plain blue sky
      note: The background is quiet and open, and nothing threatening is in
          view. Any risk lives in the figure's expectation, not in what the
          card shows.
    - element: Small green leaves budding on many of the staffs
      note: The wands are still living wood. The effort behind the figure has
          been long, and the card shows it as still growing rather than spent.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as staying on guard after
    long effort, close to the end of a stretch and wary of one more setback.
    The row of wands behind the figure shows how much has been done, and the
    watchful stance shows the strain of keeping it. In Wands, the suit of will
    and persistence, the Nine is resilience with its cost included, worn but
    still standing. It can be read as hard-won strength or as guardedness that
    has outlasted its reason.
invitationPrompt: What are you still watching for today, and is it still
    there?
