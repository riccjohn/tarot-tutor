name: The Lovers
symbols:
    - element: A nude man and a nude woman stand apart on open ground, one on each side of the card, with a gap between them
      note: They do not touch. Each stands at the edge of the picture with an open hand turned toward the centre, and the space between them is part of the composition. It is a scene of two people facing a decision as much as a scene of romance.
    - element: A large winged figure with flame-coloured hair, arms spread wide, hovering above them on a bank of cloud
      note: Its hands are raised over both people at once, and its face is turned toward neither. Conventionally read as blessing, or as a higher vantage point from which the choice is seen.
    - element: A large yellow sun with radiating lines, directly behind the winged figure
      note: The light falls from the top of the card down over the whole scene. Often read as clarity, and as something that shines on both figures equally.
    - element: A tree with red fruit and a snake coiled around its trunk behind the woman, and a tree with flame-shaped leaves behind the man
      note: Each person stands beside one tree, and the two trees are different. Often read as two kinds of desire, or two paths, that each person is turned toward.
    - element: A single rose-red mountain rising in the gap between the two figures, in the middle distance
      note: The mountain is the one thing the two figures share and neither stands on it. Conventionally read as a summit or a goal that a shared decision is aimed toward.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading centres this card on choice, with values made concrete by being chosen. It is often read as more about alignment than about romance. Two people stand on either side of an open space with something above them and something ahead of them, and neither is shown having decided. It sits late in its row, just before the push forward, so it can read as the decision that the next card acts on.
invitationPrompt: What choice in front of you is really a question about what you value?
