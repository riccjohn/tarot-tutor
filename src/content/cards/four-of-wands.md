name: Four of Wands
symbols:
    - element: Four tall wooden staffs standing upright, two on each side, with
          leaves at their tips
      note: Four is the first number that builds an enclosure, and here the
          wands are its corner posts. Nothing is drawn between them at ground
          level, so the structure is open to walk through.
    - element: A thick garland of green leaves and red flowers hung between
          the two pairs of staffs, tied on with red ribbons at the outer
          poles
      note: The garland makes the four wands into a canopy. It is the mark of
          a celebration, and it is decoration rather than defence.
    - element: Two figures in the middle ground, one in white with a blue
          drape and one in a dark red robe, each raising a bouquet
      note: They stand together under the canopy and hold their flowers up.
          Their gesture is the point of the scene, a shared and visible
          acknowledgment of something finished.
    - element: A small group of figures at the lower left edge, between the
          two left-hand poles
      note: Others are gathered at the edge of the celebration. The event
          is public rather than private, and the scene is bigger than the
          two figures at the centre.
    - element: A grey castle wall and a round tower with a red conical roof,
          behind the figures against a plain yellow sky
      note: The wall shows a settled, protected place. The wands and garland
          stand in front of it, so the celebration happens at the gate
          rather than inside the fortress.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a piece of work
    completed and the chance to celebrate it. The four posts and garland make a
    space built for marking an arrival, such as a project finished, a home
    established, or a milestone reached alongside other people. In Wands, the
    suit of drive, the Four is a rare card where the effort pauses, and it
    asks what the achievement has made possible. The steadiness here is
    welcoming more than closed off.
invitationPrompt: What has been completed lately that you haven't yet stopped
    to mark?
