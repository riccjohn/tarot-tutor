---
name: Death
symbols:
    - element: A skeletal rider in black armour and a plumed helmet, mounted
          on a white horse that steps forward with one raised hoof
      note: The rider is the card's personification of ending, and the
          conventional figure for transformation rather than for anything
          that happens to one person's body. The horse is white and
          unhurried, so the advance reads as steady, not violent.
    - element: A black banner carried on a staff, bearing a white
          five-petalled rose and the numeral XIII
      note: A flower on the emblem of endings is the card's central
          pairing. It is usually read as something that renews or blooms,
          still sitting inside the change.
    - element: A figure in a spotted cloak lying on the ground under the
          horse, with a gold crown fallen beside him
      note: The crown on the ground shows a position or role set down. This
          is conventionally read as titles and status not surviving the
          change in the way they were held.
    - element: A figure in a tall yellow mitre and robe standing with
          hands raised in front of the horse, and two smaller figures
          kneeling beside him
      note: These figures meet the rider in different ways, one standing
          and open-handed, the others kneeling, which is often read as the
          range of ways people meet an ending.
    - element: A sun rising between two pale towers in the background, above
          a river with a small boat
      note: The sun sits on the horizon in the middle of the scene, the only
          bright point in an otherwise sombre picture, and it is placed
          exactly where the rider is heading.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading takes this card as ending, release, and
    change. A chapter, a role, or a way of seeing something closes so that
    something else can begin. The sunrise between the towers and the white
    rose on the banner both point past the ending to what follows it. It sits
    just before Temperance in the sequence, which suits an ending followed by
    a recombination, and it is read as a threshold to cross, not a place
    to stop.
invitationPrompt: What is one role, habit, or story you have carried that
    you might be ready to set down?
