name: Four of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: A figure in a dark red robe and black fur-edged cloak, facing
          the viewer, with both arms wrapped around a coin held against the
          chest
      note: The grip is total, with both arms and the whole torso closed
          around one coin. The suit's emblem is held as tightly as it can be
          held, and nothing is being offered outward.
    - element: A second coin balanced on the figure's head, above a small
          gold crown
      note: The coin sits where a thought or a title would. It is often read
          as the mind occupied by what is owned, so that keeping it is a
          constant piece of attention.
    - element: A coin under each of the figure's feet, one beneath each red
          boot
      note: Four coins in all, and each is either held or stood on. Chest,
          head, and both feet form a closed arrangement, matching the Four's
          structure of four corners.
    - element: A walled city of towers and red roofs on the horizon behind the
          figure, small and to either side
      note: The town is close but the figure stands apart from it, in front,
          with an empty grey ground between. The people and the market are
          in view without being engaged.
    - element: The roman numeral IV at the top center of a plain grey sky
      note: The sky is empty, so the figure and the coins are the whole
          subject of the card.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as holding tightly to what
    has been built. The Four's stability can be a foundation or a closed-in
    structure, and in Pentacles it is most often taken as security kept close
    — savings, position, or a habit of control. The reading runs two ways. It
    can describe prudence that protects something earned, or a grip so firm
    that nothing can move through it, including new opportunity and other
    people. Which of these fits is the reader's to decide.
invitationPrompt: What are you holding onto right now, and what does it give
    you to keep hold of it?
