name: The Hierophant
symbols:
    - element: A robed figure in red sits enthroned between two grey pillars, wearing a tall gold triple crown, with one hand raised in a gesture of blessing
      note: He faces the viewer directly and speaks from a fixed seat. Conventionally read as a teacher, or as teaching that is handed down through a shared form instead of discovered alone.
    - element: A gold staff topped with a triple cross, held in his other hand
      note: The staff has three crossbars stacked one above another. Often read as layers of authority or levels of teaching, and it is the emblem of an institution rather than one person.
    - element: Two kneeling figures in the foreground, seen from behind, in patterned robes with yellow bands
      note: One robe is patterned with red roses and the other with white blossoms. They face him and their heads are bowed, so the card shows teaching as a relationship between someone who gives it and someone who receives it.
    - element: Two crossed keys lying on the red carpet at his feet
      note: The keys cross at the centre of the base of the picture. Conventionally read as access to what is closed, and sometimes as one key for the outer, conscious world and one for the inner.
    - element: A long white band down the front of his robe marked with small crosses, and small crosses on his white shoes
      note: The same mark repeats on his clothing, so belonging is expressed in the uniform as well as in the crown. Often read as the visible signs of a shared tradition.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as tradition and belonging, and as the question of which inherited rules a person accepts. He is an institution rather than a single ruler, and the two kneeling figures suggest that a teaching only exists between the person who gives it and the person who receives it. It sits before the Lovers, a card of choice, and a choice needs something to choose against. On this reading he stands for the shared framework that a person can join, question, or leave.
invitationPrompt: Which received idea have you never examined closely, and what would you find if you did?
