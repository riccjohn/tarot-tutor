name: Six of Swords
symbols:
    - element: A figure in an orange tunic standing at the left of a small boat,
          seen from behind, pushing a long dark pole into the water
      note: The figure is doing the work of moving the boat and faces the
          direction of travel. In the number's terms this is the helper, the
          one who is carrying someone else through.
    - element: Two hunched figures seated in the boat, wrapped in orange and
          grey hooded cloaks, their faces hidden
      note: Their heads are bowed and covered. Conventionally read as
          someone carrying a heavy stretch behind them, quiet and
          protected, being taken somewhere by another's effort.
    - element: Six upright swords standing point-up in the bow of the boat,
          two on the left and four on the right
      note: The blades travel with the passengers rather than being left
          behind. They are set upright and steady, which reads as a load
          being carried along, not a weapon being drawn.
    - element: Calmer pale blue water on the far side of the boat, with a low
          treed shoreline in the distance, and rippled dark water beneath the
          bow
      note: The water is rougher near the boat and flatter ahead. The card
          shows a crossing in progress from one state toward another, and
          the far shore is small but visible.
    - element: A plain grey sky with the numeral VI at the top
      note: No weather and no event; the sky leaves all attention on the
          boat and the water it is crossing.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a quiet crossing away
    from trouble, with the lessons carried along with the load. The Six
    follows the strain of the Five with relief, and in Swords that relief
    is a passage rather than a celebration — leaving a hard place, or a
    hard argument, with the help of someone who is willing to steer.
    Readers often note that the swords are still in the boat, so the
    difficulty has not vanished. It is being carried somewhere calmer.
    What is being carried, and to which shore, is left for the reader to
    say.
invitationPrompt: What are you carrying with you out of the last difficult
    stretch, and who or what is helping you carry it?
