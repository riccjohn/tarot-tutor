name: Knight of Swords
symbols:
    - element: A rider in full plate armour on a pale horse, both charging to
          the left across the card, the rider leaning far forward
      note: The whole composition tilts in one direction and everything
          points where the horse is running. This is the Knight's defining
          trait, direction with commitment behind it.
    - element: A sword held high in the rider's raised right hand, blade
          pointing up and back toward the top left corner
      note: The sword is raised, not lowered at a target. It is thought as
          the thing being carried into the charge, brandished more than
          aimed.
    - element: A red cloak and a red plume streaming backward from the rider,
          and a flowing pale mane blowing back from the horse's neck
      note: The cloak, plume, and mane all stream behind at the same angle.
          The speed is read from the fabric and hair, not from the ground,
          which barely appears.
    - element: A red heart-shaped ornament on the horse's forehead, and
          yellow butterfly-like motifs and small red birds stitched across
          its trappings
      note: A few soft, decorative details on an otherwise hard-edged
          charge. They read as the sort of small, easily overlooked things
          a fast-moving idea rides past.
    - element: Two bare trees bent hard to the left at the left edge, under a
          pale blue sky streaked with ragged grey clouds
      note: The trees lean the same way as the horse, so the wind and the
          rider are moving together. The Knight is riding with the air, the
          suit's element, not against it.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats the Knight of Swords as the stage of
    pursuit in the suit of thought, someone or something riding hard on an
    idea. The strength of the card is follow-through and the plain speed of
    a mind made up. The cost is single-mindedness, since the same drive
    that gets a point across can overshoot what the moment needed and leave
    little time to listen. As a person, this is a quick, direct speaker or
    a decisive planner; as a stage, it is the point where conviction is
    running ahead of consideration.
invitationPrompt: What idea are you moving quickly on today, and what would you
    want to hear from someone before you get there?
