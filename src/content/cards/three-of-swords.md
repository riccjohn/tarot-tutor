name: Three of Swords
symbols:
    - element: Three identical, plain-hilted swords crossing at a single point
      note: Each blade runs straight, unbroken; nothing about any one sword
          is bent, dropped, or singled out. The number is the whole event —
          three of the same thing meeting at once, not one sword doing
          something the others don't.
    - element: A single large red heart, positioned exactly where the three
          blade-tips converge and cross again just below it
      note: The heart is the only object the swords touch. Everything else
          on the card is weather and sky around that one point of contact.
    - element: Straight grey diagonal lines falling across the entire
          background, behind the heart and swords alike
      note: Conventionally read as rain. It falls evenly over the whole
          scene rather than only over the heart, so the card reads as one
          climate the heart sits inside, not a spotlight singling it out.
    - element: Two grey cloud-banks tucked into the upper left and upper
          right corners, with the roman numeral III set on a cloud between
          them at the top
      note: The clouds bookend the numeral rather than the heart, framing
          this as the third card of a sequence as much as a single image.
status: edited
commonReading: One common reading ties this card to sharp emotional pain —
    heartbreak, disappointment, or a truth that has suddenly become
    impossible to unsee. The three blades meeting at one point is often read
    as pain registering from more than one direction at once, or the same
    hurt landing in thought, gut, and feeling simultaneously rather than
    staying contained to one of them. That is one lens among several a
    reader might bring to it — plenty of readers pair this card with the
    relief that can follow clear-eyed honesty as much as with the sting of
    reaching it.
invitationPrompt: What would it mean to treat today's discomfort as clarity
    you needed, rather than damage you're managing?
