name: Three of Cups
symbols:
    - element: Three women in a tight cluster, one in a red cloak seen from
          behind, one in grey on the left, one in tan on the right
      note: Their arms are raised and overlapping, and their bodies overlap
          too. Unlike the Two's careful mirroring, the number here is a group
          pressed together, with no single figure set apart from the others.
    - element: Three golden cups raised high above their heads, at three
          different heights
      note: The cups are lifted rather than held out, in the gesture of a
          toast. The three heights make a loose triangle above the group, and
          no cup is favored over the others.
    - element: Heaped fruit at the women's feet, including orange-red fruit on
          the left, a large orange pumpkin at the lower right, and trailing
          grapes
      note: Harvest produce is the conventional sign of abundance and of
          a season's work being brought in. The celebration takes place on
          ground covered with what has been gathered.
    - element: A bunch of grapes held down at the right figure's side, and a
          wreath of leaves in her hair
      note: Grapes and wreaths belong to festival imagery. The figure holds
          the fruit almost incidentally, as someone in the middle of an
          occasion rather than posing for it.
    - element: A plain pale-blue sky with the roman numeral III at the top
      note: The empty sky leaves no threat or weather in the scene. Compared
          with the Three of Swords, the same number is drawn here as a
          gathering rather than as a crossing of blades.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as celebration shared among
    friends, a moment of gathering in which something worth marking has
    happened and people mark it together. The Three's complication is a happy
    one here, since a third person turns a pairing into a community. The raised
    cups are read as joy, generosity, and belonging, and the harvest at the
    women's feet as the fruit of some earlier effort finally being enjoyed.
invitationPrompt: Who would you raise a cup with today, and what is worth
    marking?
