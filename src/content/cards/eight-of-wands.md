---
name: Eight of Wands
symbols:
    - element: Eight wooden staffs, all slanting downward from the upper left
          to the lower right at nearly the same angle
      note: The Eight is the only Wands pip with no figure, so the number
          is the whole subject. Eight of the same thing, in parallel, gives
          the card its sense of pace and shared direction.
    - element: The staffs sit in two loose groups of four with slightly
          uneven spacing, and one tip stops short of the others
      note: The lines are not perfectly aligned, so the motion looks live
          rather than diagrammed. Several things are moving at once, close
          together and not quite in step.
    - element: Small green leaves budding along each staff and at the lower
          tips
      note: These are the same living wands as elsewhere in the suit. Even
          in full flight they carry growth with them.
    - element: A plain pale blue sky filling everything above the landscape
      note: With no clouds, figures or other objects, nothing competes with
          the staffs. The card offers a single idea, speed, and nothing else
          to look at.
    - element: A narrow strip of green hills, a stream, scattered bushes, and
          a small building on a rise at the lower left
      note: The ground is calm and still. Set against that quiet land, the
          wands read as something crossing above it, moving faster than
          anything below.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as momentum, movement and
    news arriving quickly, with several things coming together at once. The
    staffs are in flight and have not landed, so the card sits between a
    decision made and its results. In Wands, the suit of drive, the Eight is
    the stretch where effort stops feeling like effort and starts feeling like
    pace. It can be welcome as flow or feel like being carried faster than
    intended, depending on the reader.
invitationPrompt: What in your life is moving faster than you expected, and
    how does that pace feel to you?
