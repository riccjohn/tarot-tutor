---
name: Two of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: A young figure in a tall red hat, brown tunic, and red hose,
          standing on one leg in a dancing step, with a coin in each hand
      note: The pose is light and slightly off-balance, but the figure's face
          is calm and turned toward the viewer. Two coins is the whole
          number's subject, held at once with nothing set down.
    - element: A green band looped around both coins and crossing over the
          figure's chest in a figure-eight
      note: The loop is the lemniscate, the same sideways-eight shape drawn
          over the Magician and Strength. Here it is often read as a
          continuous back-and-forth, with each coin's weight passing to the
          other and back again.
    - element: Two ships on a band of blue waves behind the figure, one small
          at the left and one large and tilting on a crest at the right
      note: The ships are a second pair to the coins. Their motion is
          the card's only instability, and the figure is set in front of it
          rather than affected by it.
    - element: The waves rise and fall in a regular pattern beneath a flat
          pale sky, above a plain grey ground
      note: The sea is conventionally read as changing conditions, the
          shifting market or season that any juggling has to be done inside.
    - element: The roman numeral II at the top center
      note: This is the second card of the suit, the first pairing after the
          single coin of the Ace.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the ongoing juggling of
    two demands on the same time or money. Nothing has been chosen, and
    nothing has dropped either. The figure's easy expression is often read as
    evidence that the balance can be held with skill, while the ships riding
    the waves behind suggest that conditions keep shifting and the balancing
    has to be redone as they do. It is the Two's held balance in its
    practical form, in which two commitments are kept going at once.
invitationPrompt: Which two things are you holding at once right now, and
    what does it cost you to keep both in the air?
