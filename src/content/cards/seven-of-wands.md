---
name: Seven of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A young man in a green tunic standing on the crest of a green
          hill, his legs apart, gripping a single staff diagonally across his
          body
      note: The staff is held across the body like a guard, more than
          raised to strike. The stance is braced and defensive, the posture
          of a figure holding a position.
    - element: Six staffs pointing up at angles from below the edge of the
          hill, in front of and around the figure's feet
      note: Six wands rise from below and one is held above. The Seven is
          drawn as one against many, and the hilltop is what the figure
          stands on and guards.
    - element: A tense, frowning face turned down and to the right
      note: The figure is looking at whatever is coming up the hill, which
          the card leaves out of the picture. The challenge is present but
          unseen, so the viewer sees only the response to it.
    - element: Mismatched footwear, a low red shoe on one foot and a taller
          boot on the other
      note: The figure looks caught mid-action, not set for a fight. It is
          often read as someone defending in the middle of ordinary life,
          not a trained or fully equipped opponent.
    - element: A flat, plain blue sky with no clouds or other figures
      note: The whole card is the figure and the challengers below. Nothing in
          the background offers help, so the defence is entirely the figure's
          own.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the test of a position
    already won. The figure has the high ground and is defending it against
    challengers, and the card is more about the decision to hold than about
    the outcome. In Wands, the suit of will and ambition, that is the moment
    where commitment is measured. It can be read as steadfast conviction, or
    as the strain of defending something at all times, depending on what the
    reader brings to it.
invitationPrompt: What is worth standing your ground for today, and what
    would you be willing to let someone else have?
