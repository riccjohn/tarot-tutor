---
name: Ace of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A white hand emerging from a grey cloud at the right edge,
          gripping a single wooden staff
      note: The hand belongs to no visible figure, which is the Ace's usual
          structure across the suits, an offer held out rather than an event
          already underway.
    - element: A rust-red staff with a knobbed top, sprouting small green
          leaves along its length
      note: A living wand rather than a cut one. Leaves are the suit's
          signature, tying drive to growth as much as to force.
    - element: About ten loose green leaves hanging in the air around the
          staff, unattached to any branch
      note: Often read as energy in motion, a beginning still shedding sparks
          before it has settled into a direction.
    - element: A ring of short radiating lines around the gripping hand
      note: A conventional way of drawing a charged moment. It marks the
          grip itself, the point of commitment, as the brightest spot on the
          card.
    - element: A low green hill with a small rocky, castle-like crag beside
          it, a river, a few round trees, and distant mountains along the
          bottom edge
      note: The landscape is quiet and still, so all the movement on the
          card sits with the wand. It reads as the ground a project could
          grow into, not yet touched by it.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a spark of drive and the
    offer of a beginning, a new idea, project, or surge of energy with no
    direction chosen yet. The hand holds the staff up rather than swinging it,
    so what happens next is left open. In Wands that potential is specifically
    creative and ambitious, the wanting-to-do that comes before any plan for
    how. It is an invitation to begin and not yet a commitment to finish.
invitationPrompt: What is stirring in you today that you have not yet given a
    shape or a name?
