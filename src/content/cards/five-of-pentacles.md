name: Five of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: A stained-glass window in a stone wall, with five pentacles
          set in it in two pairs and one on top, among green leaves and
          colored panes
      note: The coins are all present and all in plain view, but as glass
          they are part of a wall, not something anyone here can hold. The
          suit's emblem is close by and out of reach at once.
    - element: A figure in a pale blue tunic on crutches, head bound in a
          cloth, looking up toward the window
      note: This is the only figure whose face is turned toward the coins.
          The crutches and bound head show a figure worn down, and the look
          upward is often read as the one who still sees where help might
          be.
    - element: A second figure in a long orange shawl, barefoot, walking
          head down and turned away from both the window and the first
          figure
      note: The two walk the same way through the same snow but do not look
          at each other. The shawl is patched and held close, so the card
          shows two people in the same hardship and not yet together in it.
    - element: Heavy snow falling across a black night, with white ground
          underfoot and no shelter drawn in front of the wall
      note: Conventionally read as cold and scarcity. The weather fills the
          whole card, and the ground is empty of anything to sit on or
          stand under.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a stretch of scarcity,
    felt as being left out in the cold. It is usually taken as financial
    strain, a period when resources have run short, or as the feeling of being
    outside something others are inside. The window matters to the reading,
    because help or shelter is shown as near enough to see. Whether the
    figures notice it, and whether they knock, is left open by the picture.
    Like the other Fives it is uncomfortable without being final, and the
    cards that follow show what recovery can look like.
invitationPrompt: Where do you feel outside something today, and what is
    close enough to reach that you may not have looked at yet?
