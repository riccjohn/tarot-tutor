name: Two of Cups
symbols:
    - element: Two figures facing each other in profile, one in a white gown
          with a blue cloak and a green wreath, the other in a patterned
          tunic with a red garland
      note: They stand on the same ground and lean toward each other with
          matching posture. Neither is placed above the other, which is the
          visual form of the Two's balance, two of something meeting as
          equals.
    - element: Two golden cups held out and nearly touching at the center of
          the card
      note: The cups meet at the exact middle, and the figures' hands almost
          meet with them. The exchange is happening in the space between the
          two people, and no liquid is spilled or shown.
    - element: A winged lion's head hovering at the top of the card, above a
          staff of two intertwined serpents standing between the cups
      note: The staff of two snakes is a familiar emblem of paired forces
          bound together. The lion's head above it is commonly read as the
          strength or passion that holds the pairing, and it watches over
          the meeting rather than taking part.
    - element: A green hill in the distance with a small house among trees,
          behind the two figures
      note: The landscape is quiet and far away, so the card's whole attention
          stays on the pair in the foreground. The house is often read as a
          shared future the two are looking toward.
    - element: The roman numeral II at the top center, under a clear blue sky
      note: The number sits directly above the point where the cups meet,
          so the card announces itself as the pairing stage of the suit.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a meeting of two, such
    as a partnership, a friendship, or a mutual attraction, in which each side
    gives and receives on equal terms. The cups meeting at the center is read
    as a feeling recognized by both people at once, with the outcome not yet
    fixed. The winged lion above the pair adds a note of energy behind the
    connection, and the pairing itself is the point of the card, before
    anything has been built on it.
invitationPrompt: Who or what are you currently meeting halfway, and what does
    each side bring?
