name: The Empress
symbols:
    - element: A woman with long fair hair sits reclined on red cushions, holding a short sceptre topped with a globe in one raised hand
      note: Her posture is relaxed rather than upright, and the sceptre is carried lightly. Conventionally read as authority exercised through ease and abundance instead of command.
    - element: A crown of stars circling a wreath of leaves
      note: A ring of small white stars sits above a green wreath. Often read as a link between the sky and the living world, and sometimes counted as twelve, one for each month.
    - element: A loose white gown printed with red pomegranates
      note: The pattern covers the whole robe, and the fruit is full of seed. Conventionally tied to fertility, abundance, and the sensory richness of things that grow.
    - element: A heart-shaped shield leaning at her side, marked with the round symbol of Venus, a circle over a cross
      note: The shield carries the sign long associated with Venus, so the card is tied to love and beauty by its own emblem.
    - element: A field of ripe yellow grain in the foreground, with a dense grove of dark trees and a stream running in from the right
      note: Grain, forest and water are all close at hand around her. Often read as the tended, physical world she sits within, at the point where growth is well underway.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading takes this card as growth, care, and the making of things real. Where the Magician and the High Priestess before her act and wait, she is the first figure of the fully tended, physical world, with grain, water, and trees all in season. She is usually paired with the Emperor at 4, one generative and one structuring. On this reading the card is about nourishing something and letting it grow at its own pace.
invitationPrompt: Where in your life is something growing that could use tending rather than managing?
