name: Ace of Cups
symbols:
    - element: A white hand open, palm up, emerging from a grey cloud at the
          right edge and holding a single golden chalice
      note: Every Ace is drawn this way, an offering with no scene around it.
          The hand does not grip the cup; it presents it, which is why the
          card is usually read as something given rather than something taken.
    - element: Several streams of water pouring over the rim of the chalice and
          falling in long lines to a pond below
      note: The cup is already overflowing at the moment it appears. Water
          is the suit's element, so the overflow reads as feeling arriving in
          more quantity than a container was built to hold.
    - element: A white dove descending from the top edge, carrying a round
          wafer marked with a cross in its beak, directly above the chalice
      note: A traditional image of blessing or grace arriving from outside the
          scene. It is the only figure in the card that is not part of the
          water or the hand, which sets it apart as the source of the gift.
    - element: Small water drops scattered in the air around the hand and
          chalice
      note: The drops fall beyond the streams, so the spilling reaches
          past the cup's own edges and into the space around it.
    - element: A pond across the bottom of the card covered in green lily pads
          with red flowers, the streams landing in its surface
      note: The overflow returns to still water. Lilies are conventionally
          tied to emotional life growing out of calm, and the pond gives the
          spilled water somewhere to go.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the arrival of a feeling
    before there are words for it, such as a new attachment, a burst of
    creative warmth, or a sudden sense of what something means to you. The
    overflowing cup is read as more than a container can hold, and the open
    hand as an offer rather than an event. What it becomes is decided by the
    cards that follow. Right now it is pure potential, with nothing yet
    committed to.
invitationPrompt: What feeling has been arriving lately that you haven't yet
    found a name for?
