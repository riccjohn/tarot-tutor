---
name: Page of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A young figure in profile, standing with the head tipped back to
          look up at the top of a tall wooden staff
      note: The Page is examining the emblem as if it were new, which is the
          rank's whole position. Attention comes first and action comes later.
    - element: A grey hat with a single red feather, over curling hair
      note: The feather is a small flourish of lightness. It gives the figure
          a young, slightly jaunty look, quite unlike the settled crowns of
          the older court cards.
    - element: A yellow tunic patterned with dark curling shapes, a
          green-gold cloak draped from the shoulders, and yellow boots with
          scalloped tops
      note: The pattern of curling shapes is often read as small salamanders,
          a conventional emblem of fire. The clothing is bright and rich, and
          it carries the suit's element.
    - element: A single staff, budding with a few green leaves and taller
          than the figure, held upright in both hands
      note: The wand is being held to study more than to wield. It is as
          tall as the person, which suits someone whose enthusiasm is larger
          than their experience.
    - element: A stretch of flat orange-red desert with three peaked orange
          hills and one small bare tree, under a pale blue sky
      note: The ground is warm, dry and open, the colour of fire's element.
          Nothing has been built here yet, and the whole landscape is space
          for something to start.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the beginner's stage of
    Wands, an early enthusiasm and a first look at what drive can do. The Page
    reads as a message, a spark of curiosity, or someone approaching
    a creative or ambitious pursuit with fewer assumptions than the people
    around them. The staff is held and studied and not yet swung, so the card
    is about attention before action, with interest running ahead of skill. That
    gap is part of the position and is allowed to be there.
invitationPrompt: What have you just started, or nearly started, that you are
    curious about and not yet good at?
