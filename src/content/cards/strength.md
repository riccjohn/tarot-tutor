name: Strength
symbols:
    - element: A woman in a white gown bends over a large red lion and rests both hands on its open jaws
      note: She is bent toward the animal and her posture is gentle, with no visible struggle. Conventionally read as influence exercised through calm and patience instead of force.
    - element: A lion with a thick curling red mane, its mouth open and its body low, tail curling behind it
      note: The lion is large, fully present, and not diminished by the scene. Often read as instinct, appetite, or strong feeling, met and worked with rather than beaten.
    - element: A horizontal figure-eight floating above her head, over a crown of flowers and leaves
      note: The same unbroken loop that appears over the Magician's head, here with a wreath beneath it. Conventionally tied to steady attention and continuity.
    - element: A long chain of red and white flowers looped from her waist and around the lion's neck
      note: The lion is joined to her by blossoms, not by iron. Often read as a connection made by affection or by agreement, in place of restraint.
    - element: A flat yellow sky with a pale blue mountain and green hills in the distance
      note: Only a small strip of open land lies behind them, and there are no onlookers. The interaction takes place in the open, and it is quiet rather than a spectacle.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading takes this card as gentle, sustained influence in place of force, the quiet kind of courage. The woman does not overpower the lion, and the lion does not withdraw. It is often read as working with one's own nature and feelings rather than against them. As the first card of the second row, it opens the inward turn toward patience and self-command.
invitationPrompt: What feeling of yours might respond better to steadiness than to being pushed away?
