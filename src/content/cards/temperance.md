---
name: Temperance
symbols:
    - element: A winged figure in a white robe with large red-and-purple
          wings, a rayed halo, and curled golden hair
      note: The wings and halo mark the figure as something other than an
          ordinary person. The figure is calm and looking down at the
          task in its hands, which sets the tone of unhurried attention.
    - element: Two gold cups held in the two hands, with a thin stream of
          blue water arcing from the upper cup into the lower one
      note: The stream is the card's action and the conventional image of
          blending or adjusting. The water stays in one unbroken arc, which
          reads as care and a steady hand.
    - element: One foot resting on the surface of a pool and the other on a
          stone at the water's edge
      note: The figure stands with one foot in water and one on land,
          conventionally read as a balance between two settings, such as
          feeling and structure, or two states being brought into contact.
    - element: A white square on the front of the robe containing an orange
          triangle
      note: The triangle inside the square is the most deliberate geometric
          mark in the picture, and it sits at the centre of the chest.
          Readers commonly take it as a form held within a frame.
    - element: A winding path running up into hills toward a bright sun
          between two peaks, and yellow irises growing tall on the right bank
      note: The path leads away from the water and out of the frame, so
          the card is oriented toward a next stage as much as toward the
          pouring itself.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as moderation, the
    patient mixing of unlike things into a workable whole. It follows an
    ending in the sequence, and it is often read as the answer to change,
    which is to adjust, combine, and go slowly. The steady stream between the
    two cups suggests that the aim is the right proportion rather than
    more or less of anything. The path behind the figure points on to what
    comes after the blending.
invitationPrompt: Which two things in your life today might do better mixed
    together, and in what proportion?
