---
name: Ace of Swords
symbols:
    - element:
          A single upright double-edged sword, its blade pointing straight up
          the centre of the card
      note: One blade, no second figure and nothing to compare it to. This is
          the suit's emblem shown on its own, before it has been used for
          anything.
    - element: A white hand emerging from a grey cloud on the left, gripping
          the hilt, with short rays radiating from the wrist
      note: The Ace's standard image across the suit. The hand offers the
          sword rather than swinging it, so the card reads as something held
          out, not something already done.
    - element: A golden crown threaded onto the tip of the blade, with a green
          olive-like branch hanging on its left and a palm-like frond on its
          right
      note: Conventionally read as victory and peace joined to the blade. The
          crown places the thought above every other element on the card.
    - element: Six small yellow drops or leaves falling in the air on either
          side of the blade below the crown
      note: Often read as a shower of light or blessing. They fall evenly on
          both sides of the sword, so they mark the moment rather than
          favouring one side.
    - element: Rugged purple and blue-green mountains along the bottom edge,
          under a plain pale grey sky
      note: The landscape is distant and empty. Nothing has been built or
          fought over yet, so the whole card is open ground.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a single clear thought
    arriving before it has been tested against anything — an insight, a
    decision, or a plain statement of what is true. The crown at the tip is
    often taken to mean that the thought carries authority once it is
    spoken. Like every Ace it is an offer more than an event, and Swords
    makes it a sharp one, because a clear idea can cut in both directions.
    What it becomes depends on the numbers that follow and on what the
    person holding it chooses to do.
invitationPrompt: What is one thing you can see clearly today that you haven't
    yet said out loud?
