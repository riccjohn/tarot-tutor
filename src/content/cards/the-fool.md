name: The Fool
symbols:
    - element: A young traveller in a patterned tunic stands on the very edge of a rocky ledge, one foot planted and the other lifted, with his face turned up toward the sky
      note: The gaze is aimed above the ground rather than at it. Conventionally read as trust or absorption in something beyond the next step, and the drop beneath the lifted foot is left plainly visible.
    - element: A small bundle on a long staff, carried over the shoulder
      note: Everything he carries fits on one pole. Often read as travelling light, with little planned and few possessions to lean on.
    - element: A white rose held loosely in the open left hand
      note: The one delicate thing in the picture, held out rather than gripped. Conventionally tied to openness and simple intention.
    - element: A small white dog leaping at his heels, mouth open
      note: The only other living figure on the ledge. Read as companion, instinct, or a voice of caution, and it is not clear from the image which of these it is.
    - element: A large white sun with radiating rays in the upper right, over a flat yellow sky, with snow-capped peaks below
      note: The bright open sky and distant mountains set the scale. The road ahead is wide and undefined, and the numeral 0 above the traveller marks a card that begins before the count does.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a beginning made before the outcome is known, with a leap taken lightly and openly. The traveller looks up rather than down, and that is often read as trust, curiosity, or willingness to start without a full map. It is a start rather than a stage, which fits its number, 0, coming before the sequence begins. What he is stepping toward is left open, and so is what to bring.
invitationPrompt: What is one thing you could begin today without knowing how it ends?
