name: The Hanged Man
symbols:
    - element: A figure hanging upside down by one foot, the head at the
          bottom of the card, in a blue tunic, red leggings, and yellow shoes
      note: The whole card is inverted, so the viewer has to take the
          figure's angle to read the face. The suspension is the point of the
          image, and the card's conventional theme of seeing from a changed
          position starts here.
    - element: One straight red leg attached to the beam, the other leg bent
          behind it so the two form a figure four
      note: Only one leg is fixed. The crossed leg gives the body a
          deliberate, arranged shape, which is why the pose reads as chosen
          rather than as a struggle.
    - element: A yellow halo with radiating strokes around the head, and a
          calm, open-eyed face
      note: Conventionally read as insight or a settled mind. The face shows
          no strain, which sets the mood of the whole card as waiting
          rather than distress.
    - element: A bare, T-shaped wooden frame, with green leaves trailing
          from both ends of the crossbeam
      note: The beam is living wood, still putting out leaves, not a
          finished structure. It supports the reading of the pause as part of
          growth rather than a stop.
    - element: A plain beige-grey field with no landscape, and the numeral XII
          at the top edge
      note: Nothing else is present to compare the figure against. The only
          thing that has changed is the position from which he is seen.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a voluntary pause and a
    change of perspective, seeing a situation more clearly by declining to
    act on it for a moment. The upside-down figure holds still while the
    world tips around him, and the halo suggests the stillness is
    productive. Sitting between Justice and Death in the sequence, the card
    is often read as the step between a weighing and an ending, when
    the plan is set down before anything changes.
invitationPrompt: If you turned a situation you are holding on its head,
    what would you see that you did not see before?
