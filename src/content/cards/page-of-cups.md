name: Page of Cups
symbols:
    - element: A young figure standing with one hand on the hip and the head
          tilted, wearing a blue hat with a trailing scarf
      note: The pose is relaxed and a little theatrical, weight shifted onto
          one leg. Unlike the Knight or the Queen, the Page is drawn in an
          easy, unhurried stance, looking with interest rather than acting.
    - element: A golden cup held out in one hand, with a small blue fish
          poking its head up over the rim
      note: The fish is the surprise the cup contains, and the Page looks at
          it with a slight smile. A fish rising out of a vessel of water is
          the card's image of a feeling or idea surfacing unexpectedly, and
          the Page takes it in as it comes rather than trying to name it.
    - element: A blue tunic patterned with pink and white lotus-like flowers,
          over pink sleeves and pink leggings
      note: Flowers are a soft, receptive pattern, and the water-flower motif
          echoes the suit's element. The whole outfit is worn as decoration
          rather than as armor, which sets the tone of the card as gentle
          rather than guarded.
    - element: Blue waves running across the middle of the background, with
          a flat yellow shore beneath the figure's feet
      note: The sea is behind the Page and not around the Page, so the
          suit's element is held at a small distance. The Page stands on dry
          ground beside it, close enough to draw something out and not yet
          in it.
    - element: A plain grey sky with the card's title in the bottom border
      note: Nothing else competes with the figure and the cup, so all the
          card's attention goes to the small exchange between the Page and
          the fish.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats the Page of Cups as a message or an
    early curiosity about feeling, the first look at what the suit can do.
    The fish rising from the cup is read as an intuition, a creative idea, or a
    tender impulse arriving before the Page knows what to make of it. Readers
    apply the card to a new attachment, a moment of imaginative play, or a
    willingness to feel something without yet knowing where it leads. The Page
    is learning to feel and to say so, and the card shows that beginning with
    delight rather than pressure.
invitationPrompt: What small, unexpected feeling has surfaced for you lately,
    and what happens if you simply look at it?
