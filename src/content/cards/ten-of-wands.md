---
name: Ten of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A figure in a red tunic and pale leggings, bent forward at the
          waist and walking to the right, with the head down against the
          bundle
      note: The face is hidden behind the load, so the card shows the
          carrying and not the person. The posture reads as effort sustained
          over a distance, and the figure is moving, not stopped.
    - element: Ten wooden staffs gathered in one armful, their tops fanning
          out above the figure's back and their lower ends trailing down
          in front
      note: This is the full count of the suit, all in one bundle. The Ace was
          a single wand offered in one hand, and the Ten is the same emblem
          multiplied until it fills the card.
    - element: Small green leaves still growing from the staffs
      note: Even at the heaviest point in the suit the wands are living wood.
          The load is made of ambitions and projects that were once each
          carried gladly.
    - element: A small cluster of buildings with a red roof, and a few green
          trees, at the lower right
      note: A destination is in view, toward which the figure is walking. The
          card shows the load together with a place to set it down.
    - element: A wide flat ochre path across the bottom, under a plain blue
          sky
      note: The ground is level and unobstructed. The difficulty on the card
          is the weight itself, not the road.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as a burden of commitments
    carried further than one person needs to carry them. The bundle is made
    of things the figure took on, and the town ahead suggests the effort is
    close to done. In Wands, the suit of drive, the Ten is what ambition
    becomes when nothing has been set down along the way. It can be read as
    responsibility worth its weight or as a prompt to ask which of the ten
    are actually yours to carry.
invitationPrompt: Of everything you are carrying right now, what could be
    handed to someone else or put down?
