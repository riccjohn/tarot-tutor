name: Page of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: A young figure in a green tunic and a red hooded cap, standing
          upright on a meadow with both hands raised
      note: The stance is still and attentive. This is the Page's usual
          position, in which the figure stops and looks at the suit's
          emblem as something new.
    - element: A single pentacle held up in both hands at face height, with
          the figure's eyes fixed on it
      note: The coin is not being spent or counted. It is being examined,
          turned toward the light like an object whose worth has yet to be
          learned. All the attention on the card is on that one coin.
    - element: A meadow of short grass with small red and yellow flowers
          underfoot, and a few trees at the far left
      note: The ground is green and lightly planted, with nothing built on it.
          It suits a Page of Pentacles, in whom the suit's concern with
          growth and craft is only beginning.
    - element: A plowed field of red furrows and a pale blue mountain in the
          right distance
      note: Worked land and a far horizon are both in view. The furrows show
          the labor that could come from this coin, and the mountain a long
          way to go.
    - element: A flat yellow sky with no clouds, filling the upper card
      note: The background is empty, which leaves the figure and coin alone
          as the subject, in the same way the Page's own attention is
          narrowed to one thing.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the beginner's stage of
    the suit, a student's careful first look at work, money, or a craft. The
    Page examines the coin instead of using it, so the card is often read as
    study, a plan being learned, or a practical opportunity being taken
    seriously before anything is committed. As a person, it is the apprentice
    or newcomer who pays close attention to how something is done. The field
    behind is already plowed, which readers take as a sign that the ground is
    ready for the work even though it hasn't started.
invitationPrompt: What are you studying closely right now that you are not yet
    ready to put to use?
