name: Queen of Pentacles
symbols:
    - element: A crowned woman in a red gown with white sleeves and a green
          mantle, seated on a heavy carved stone throne, head bowed
      note: She sits in the Queen's usual pose, at rest inside her own
          territory. The bowed head and the settled posture put the card's
          weight on attention, not display.
    - element: A large pentacle resting across her lap, cradled in both
          hands
      note: She holds it as one holds something small and living, close
          against her body. The suit's emblem is under her care, neither
          offered out nor guarded away.
    - element: An arch of green vines and red roses across the top of the
          card, framing her on both sides
      note: The canopy makes a garden room around the throne. The growth
          is her doing, in the sense that the card places her in a place she
          has cultivated and lives in.
    - element: A small brown rabbit in the grass at the lower right, and
          red flowers growing at her feet and beside the throne
      note: The rabbit is conventionally read as fertility and ease. It sits
          unafraid close to the throne, so the animal treats the Queen's
          ground as safe.
    - element: Blue hills and a stream in the distance behind her, under a
          plain yellow sky
      note: The wider landscape is present but far off. The Queen sits in
          the near, tended ground and does not look toward the view.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as the suit's energy
    embodied, a practical warmth that looks after a home, a household, or a
    livelihood. The Queen is the stage where mastery of Pentacles has become
    second nature, and here that means comfort, generosity, and steady
    provision. The coin in her lap and the garden around her are read as a
    person for whom looking after the material world is a form of care for the
    people in it. As a role, she is the one who keeps a place or a project
    running well through attention to detail. The reading is not tied to
    gender, and it often describes an inner stance more than a person.
invitationPrompt: What in your surroundings have you tended so well that it
    now looks after you in return?
