---
name: Knight of Wands
symbols:
    - element: A rider in steel armour on a rearing chestnut horse that
          faces left, its front legs lifted off the ground
      note: The horse is at the top of a leap and the rider is turned toward
          the viewer. Direction and speed are what define the Knight rank, and
          here the movement is sudden more than steady.
    - element: A tall wooden staff held upright in the rider's raised hand,
          with a few green leaves at its top and along its side
      note: The wand is carried like a banner, not swung like a weapon. It is
          a living staff, so the drive is still tied to growth.
    - element: A helmet crowned with a large plume of flame-red feathers
      note: The plume streams behind the rider like a torch. It is a
          conventional fire image, and it makes the enthusiasm of the card
          visible from a distance.
    - element: A yellow surcoat over the armour, patterned with dark
          crescent-like curls and trimmed with a ragged flame-shaped hem
      note: The same curling pattern appears on the Page of Wands. The
          repeated emblem ties the two figures together, marking them as the
          same suit at different stages.
    - element: A flat desert of orange sand with three small, pointed hills at
          the lower left, under a pale blue sky
      note: The terrain is open and dry, and it is the same landscape as the
          rest of the court. Nothing lies between the rider and the far edge
          of the card.
status: review
commonReading: One common reading treats this card as Wands energy in full
    pursuit, a person or phase driven by conviction, quick to act and quick to
    commit. The rearing horse and streaming plume show momentum that has not
    yet been slowed by second thoughts. The strength of the rank is follow-
    through, and its cost can be overshooting what the moment needed. In
    Wands the pursuit is of a project, a vision, or a change of place, and it
    is about doing more than deliberating.
invitationPrompt: Where do you feel an urge to move quickly today, and what
    would you notice if you rode a little slower?
