numeral: 10
stage: The cycle complete, and its weight fully felt.

Ten completes the sequence that began with the Ace. The Ace is a single
emblem; the Ten is the full weight of it, ten wands, cups, swords, or
pentacles arranged across one card. It is the point where the suit's energy
has been carried as far as it goes, and the composition usually shows a figure
or figures at the far end of that load.

One common reading treats the Ten as a completion that can arrive as
fulfillment or as overload. The suit is fully expressed, and anything new
will have to begin from here. It lands differently in each suit. In Wands it
is a burden of commitments carried too far, often read as taking on more than
one person needs to hold; in Cups it is emotional fulfillment, a shared and
settled happiness; in Swords it is the exhausted end of a hard stretch, where
the worst has passed and the ground is clear again; in Pentacles it is lasting
security, resources shared across a family or community. The throughline stays
the same: the cycle has run its full length, and a Ten leaves the reader at
the threshold of the next Ace.
